# Insights Summary (Key Findings)

## RFM Segmentation

RFM segmentation reveals clear differences in customer purchasing
behavior.

Key findings:

• Champions and Loyal customers generate the highest revenue per
customer.

• Potential Loyalists represent the largest customer segment and
contribute significant overall revenue.

• At Risk and Hibernating customers indicate potential churn and should
be targeted with retention campaigns.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Cohort Retention

Cohort analysis highlights customer retention trends.

Major observations:

• Retention typically drops significantly after the first purchase
period.

• Customers who remain active after early months show stronger long‑term
engagement.

• Improving the first customer experience can increase long‑term
retention.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Customer Lifetime Value (CLV Proxy)

CLV proxy scoring identifies the most valuable customers.

Key insights:

• A small percentage of customers generate a large share of revenue.

• High‑value customers should be prioritized for loyalty programs and
targeted engagement.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Market Basket Analysis

Market basket analysis identifies products frequently purchased
together.

Insights include:

• Common product pair combinations suggest strong cross‑sell
opportunities.

• Retailers can increase sales using bundle promotions and product
placement strategies.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

## Strategic Recommendations

Based on the analysis:

• Focus marketing on high‑value customer segments.\
• Implement retention campaigns for At Risk customers.\
• Improve early customer engagement.\
• Use product pair insights for bundle promotions.

These strategies can increase customer lifetime value and improve
overall revenue.
